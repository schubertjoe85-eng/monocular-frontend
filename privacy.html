Monocular Privacy Policy Monocular collects the following information: - Images, 3D models, and prompts you submit, solely to generate AI-assisted architectural visualisations. - Your email address, if you create an account or purchase render credits. - A subscription identifier, if you subscribe via Apple's in-app purchase. - Basic usage and diagnostic information, to improve application performance and reliability. To provide the service, this information is shared with the following processors, solely for the purpose of delivering your request: - OpenAI, to generate photorealistic still images. - Runway, to generate video renders. - RevenueCat, to manage and verify Apple subscriptions. - Stripe, to process credit purchases. - Supabase, to store your account, credit balance, and render history. Monocular does not sell your data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to upload any images or models submitted to the service.

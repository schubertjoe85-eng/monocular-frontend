Monocular Desktop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Monocular Desktop renders architectural visualisations entirely on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your 3D models, drawing-set PDFs, prompts, and the images and videos you render are processed on your Mac. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Drawing sets are read on your Mac using Apple's on-device text recognition.
  • The app keeps its settings, and a cache of site information you have looked up, on your Mac only.

What is sent over the internet

When you enter a site address and press Find, the app looks up information about that place. Only the site's coordinates (and, for geocoding, the address text you typed) are sent. Nothing that identifies you is sent.

  • Apple Maps / MapKit (Apple) converts the address to coordinates and provides satellite and map imagery. It is subject to Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap, via the Overpass API provides building outlines, heights, trees and street lamps around the site.
  • Open-Meteo.com provides terrain elevation (Copernicus DEM) and current weather (cloud cover and visibility) for the site.

These requests go directly from your Mac to those services. We do not receive or store them.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Weather and elevation data by Open-Meteo.com.

What we do not do

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not collect, store or sell your data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking.

Your content

You are responsible for having the right to use any models, drawings and images you load into the app.

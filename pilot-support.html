Pilot Live Map Support Pilot shows live aircraft, weather radar and cloud cover, and emergency incidents across Australia on a single map. Frequently asked questions: - The map is blank or layers aren't loading: check your internet connection and try the Refresh button. Live data refreshes automatically every 30 seconds. - Weather card not showing: zoom in on an area (weather only appears once you're zoomed in close enough on a location). - Location permission: Pilot only uses your location on-device to centre the map; it is never sent anywhere.

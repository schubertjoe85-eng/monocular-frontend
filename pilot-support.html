PILOT SUPPORT

Pilot is a live map of aircraft, official emergency incidents and weather, worldwide. Spot a plane overhead and tap it to look it up.

The status says STALE, or nothing is moving

The strip at the top shows how many seconds ago aircraft and incidents last updated. Aircraft refresh every 10 seconds and incidents every minute. If it says STALE, check your internet connection, then tap the refresh button at the bottom right.

No aircraft where I am

Aircraft come from a community network of ADS-B receivers. Coverage is very good around cities and airports and thinner over remote land and oceans. Aircraft that aren't broadcasting ADS-B, or are out of range of a receiver, won't appear.

Which incidents are shown?

Worldwide: GDACS disaster alerts, USGS earthquakes and NASA natural events. In Australia: ACT, NSW, Victoria, Queensland and WA emergency services. In the USA: National Weather Service alerts. In the UK: Environment Agency flood warnings. In New Zealand: GeoNet earthquakes. Regional services load when the map is over their area. Tap ⓘ Sources in the app for the full list.

Notifications

Pilot alerts you when a new incident starts within 25 km of you, or an aircraft squawks an emergency code, while the app is open. Allow notifications when asked, or later in Settings > Notifications > Pilot.

Location

Location centres the map and measures distances. It stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

Pilot Live Map Privacy Policy Pilot does not require an account and does not collect or store any personal information on a server. - Location: if you grant permission, your device's location is used only on-device to centre the map. It is never transmitted to us or stored. - No account, email, or payment information is collected. - No user data is sold or shared with third parties, and none is used for advertising or tracking. Pilot displays live public data fetched directly from third-party providers as you use the map: - OpenSky Network, for live aircraft positions. - Open-Meteo, for weather conditions and forecasts. - RainViewer, for radar imagery. - OpenWeatherMap, for cloud cover imagery. - ACT Emergency Services Agency and NSW Rural Fire Service, for public emergency incident feeds. These requests go directly from your device to each provider and are subject to that provider's own privacy policy.
